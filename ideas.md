# CG Tennis OS™ — Design Directions

## Three possible approaches

### The Clay Court Journal
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial, tactile world inspired by sun-warmed clay courts, coaching notebooks, and considered long-form thinking. It positions CG Tennis OS™ as a body of work rather than another coaching platform.

**Probability:** 0.07

### The Quiet Baseline
**Very Brief Intro:** A restrained, architectural system where disciplined typographic hierarchy and court-line geometry carry the brand. It feels deliberate, practical, and quietly premium.

**Probability:** 0.04

### The Clubhouse Almanac
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, illustrated coaching field guide that combines human story with clear operating tools. CourtToon-style character illustrations and annotated tennis motifs make the brand immediately recognisable without becoming playful for its own sake.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach — The Clubhouse Almanac

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial illustration meets British sporting ephemera: the visual language of a beautifully produced coaching manual, distilled into a modern digital front door.

### Core Principles
1. **Human authority before product claims:** Caroline’s experience and voice lead, with the platform presented as the practical expression of that expertise.
2. **Guidance, not noise:** Every element should clarify a choice, a thought, or a next step; no decorative interface clutter.
3. **Tennis in the details:** Court markings, score-card framing, service boxes, and coaching annotations create the system’s recognisable vocabulary.
4. **Warm precision:** Soft, lived-in materials and friendly illustration sit alongside rigorous typographic structure and generous breathing room.

### Color Philosophy
The palette begins with a **signature court clay**, an unmistakable burnt orange that signals lived experience and momentum rather than generic sports energy. Deep ink blue gives the work intellectual weight; cream and chalk provide the reading surface of a coaching notebook; restrained grass green acts as an occasional signal of player growth. The site should feel sun-warmed, tactile, and credible rather than glossy or tech-forward.

### Layout Paradigm
Pages behave like an annotated coaching almanac: a vertical editorial journey broken by off-centre side notes, ruled dividers, and asymmetric blocks. Content progresses along a notional court baseline rather than collecting in uniform, centred card grids.

### Signature Elements
1. **CourtToon cameos:** Warm, confident illustrated coach figures punctuate high-attention areas and deliver human recognition.
2. **The coaching margin:** Narrow annotated sidebars, small running labels, and line-number-like details frame larger editorial copy.
3. **Court geometry:** Fine chalk court lines, rally arcs, and score-block compositions are used sparingly as dividers, not as decoration.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interaction should feel like turning to the right marked page in a well-used coaching notebook. Links underline with a quick, confident sweep; cards lift only slightly; prompts reveal the next relevant action without interrupting reading.

### Animation
Use low-key motion at 160–260ms with a crisp ease-out. Court lines can draw into view as sections enter; illustration accents can settle upward by a few pixels with an opacity fade. Staggered content groups use 50ms intervals. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never make reading-dependent content animate continuously.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** provides reflective authority for major statements, page titles, and pull quotes. **Manrope** provides functional clarity for navigation, labels, long-form body content, and interface actions. Display type is reserved for consequential thought; body type remains compact, open, and highly readable. Utility labels are uppercase Manrope with generous tracking.

### Brand Essence
**CG Tennis OS™ is the coaching operating system that turns hard-won court wisdom into repeatable player development for ambitious tennis coaches.**

**Personality:** Assured, generous, clear-eyed.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct, occasionally challenging, and never inflated. CTAs are invitations to assess or apply a better way of coaching. Microcopy speaks as one coach to another: intelligent, specific, and unforced.

**Example lines:**
- “Good coaching should compound.”
- “Put your judgement to work — not under admin.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a **single, bold tennis-ball arc intersecting an open coaching notebook**: an abstract symbol of movement meeting structure. It is used as a strong, text-free icon, paired with a custom tracked CG Tennis OS™ wordmark set in Manrope.

### Signature Brand Color
**Court Clay — #C95D38**

## Style Decisions

- CourtToon illustrations should portray Caroline as warm, confident, and relaxed — never as a generic corporate avatar or photographic substitute.
- CTAs that begin a trial must send visitors to `https://cgtennisos.com`; pricing should not be duplicated on this companion site.
- The Insights hub should visibly distinguish **Starter** articles for first contact from **Deep Dive** diagnostic content, with conversion prompts reserved for the conclusion of the latter.

- Every route carries at least one visible coaching-margin device: a numbered note, ruled sidebar, court-line annotation, score-card rail, or direct field-log framing.
- Insight cards are coaching scorecards. Starter and Deep Dive pieces use field-guide stamps and ruled metadata, with a featured diagnostic card interrupting the default grid rhythm.
- Grass green is reserved for the growth-oriented Starter-read signal. Court Clay carries the primary actions and editorial emphasis everywhere else.
- Every primary route includes an integrated human illustration moment — Caroline, a coaching cameo, or a CourtToon field-guide figure — so human authority is visible before product claims.
- The global header behaves as a clubhouse masthead: a distinctive notebook-and-arc mark, custom tracked wordmark, field index reference, and a quiet court-clay rule replace generic navigation cues.
- Insights filters and cards use stamped, ruled scorecard language. The tier split is signalled by field-guide markings rather than ordinary content-library controls.

### Organic Blueprint — Information Architecture

The principal navigation must foreground four growth routes: a problem-solving home page that identifies the system for coaches, players and clubs; **Insights**, the search-led coaching problem library; **Updates**, a public development log with stable, shareable individual entries; and an intentionally deferred self-assessment pathway. The future quiz is presented as a useful promise rather than a form, subscription mechanism, or unfinished interaction until its concept and email-data handling are confirmed.
